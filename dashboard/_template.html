The question this page answers

“If we open one more restaurant, where?” A locality is attractive when demand is already proven (high votes per restaurant = people go out and review there) but the incumbent quality bar is low (a beatable average rating). Score = z(log demand) − z(avg rating), localities with 15+ restaurants only.

Opportunity quadrant

X = incumbent average rating. Y = votes per restaurant (log scale). Top-left = proven demand, weak competition.
